# Titan Markets Fed Pivot Playbook

## Cover

**Fed Pivot Playbook**  
Historical 45-day sector alpha framework for anticipated policy shifts

Prepared for Titan Entry and institutional consulting use. The model ranks equity sectors by their consistency of alpha generation versus SPY in the 45 trading days before and after Federal Reserve policy-rate changes.

## 1. Executive View

Fed policy changes create a repeatable event window, but leadership depends on direction and timing. The Titan Markets Sector Rotation Model separates pre-decision positioning from post-decision confirmation so the tactical process does not mix anticipation effects with policy-reaction effects.

For expected H2 2026 cuts, the playbook emphasizes post-cut consistency first, then pre-cut confirmation when inflation, labor-market, credit, and breadth data align.

## 2. Methodology

Universe: SPDR sector ETFs measured against SPY.

Window: 45 trading days before and 45 trading days after each detected Fed funds rate change.

Alpha: sector ETF price return minus SPY price return over the same window.

Consistency score: composite of average alpha, hit rate, alpha volatility, and drawdown control.

Event source: FRED effective federal funds rate. Price source: Yahoo Finance public chart data.

## 4. Recommended Allocation

The base-case allocation is built from the top six post-cut sectors by composite score. The sleeve is normalized to 100% and capped so no single sector exceeds 25%.

The recommended use is as a tactical overlay, not a replacement for strategic equity allocation.

## 5. Risk Guardrails

Position sizing: 15-25% cap per sector ETF inside the tactical sleeve.

Market risk: reduce the sleeve by one-third if SPY closes below its 100-day moving average during the event window.

Rate risk: pause rotation if the 10-year Treasury yield rises more than 50 bps while cuts are being priced.

False pivot risk: require labor-market or inflation confirmation before increasing exposure ahead of the decision.

Client risk: Titan Entry accounts should use narrower sizing than institutional mandates with explicit IPS approval.

## 6. Deployment Calendar

T minus 45 trading days: activate monitoring, rank sectors, and stage model allocations.

T minus 20 trading days: confirm Fed probabilities, inflation trajectory, credit spreads, and earnings revisions.

Decision week: execute only if policy signal and market breadth agree.

T plus 45 trading days: harvest alpha, rebalance, or extend only with fresh signal confirmation.

## 7. Institutional Consulting Notes

For institutional clients, use the model as a research sleeve inside an approved asset-allocation framework. Adapt sector caps, benchmark constraints, tracking-error bands, liquidity screens, and reporting cadence to the client mandate.

## 8. Disclosures

Backtests use observable historical data and do not include transaction costs, taxes, slippage, advisory fees, or total-return reinvestment.

The 45-day window is a tactical research convention, not a guarantee that future Fed cycles will behave similarly.

Sector ETFs may not match client restrictions or institutional benchmarks; use approved vehicles and mandate-specific limits.

Research inputs should be refreshed before every client-facing recommendation.
